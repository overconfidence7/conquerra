- 2026-10-02: How maps are built: a fine mesh of a few thousand small cells → land and water from noise (a continent with bays, seas, lakes, island chains) → territories grown from spread-out seeds over a "wobbly" cost field and evened out → mountain ranges cut along territory borders, leaving passes → rivers along borders, always downhill → sea lanes (every island first, then shortcuts) → provinces → capitals → drawing data.
